This file is to show the modeling method for differnt types of motors:
It mainly covers radial flux motor type:
1. slotted motor.  
2. slotlesss motor.
Both type of motor include an insulation material wrapping or equivalent wrapping around the winding, so it works as an insulation between winding to its surrondings.
For slotted motor type: a. slot openning is relatively small so the contact between winding in slot to the airgap can be neglected. b. in the radial direction from inner to out side, so the nodes are: shaft  -> rotor yoke -> magnet on rotor surface -> airgap -> stator Tooth, stator winding is connected to stator tooth in tangential direction through liner, as well as stator yoke in radial direction through liner, and then stator tooth is directly connected to stator yoke in radial direction.
In slotless structure, there is no slot, so one side of the winding is touching the stator yoke through liner without stator tooth, the other side is touching airgap.
In both cases, liner can be customerized as a different material, so customer can specify the heat convection/conduction coefficient.
For rotor side, since the axial heat source is kind of symmetric, so the rotor thermal network is 1 slice or 2D node.
For stator side, due to the existance of end winding and end cap, so the stator side is cutted to three parts to include the end effect. The housing is contacting the axial end of motor through cap, the end cap is also conducting heat to ambient air thorugh forced air convection.
